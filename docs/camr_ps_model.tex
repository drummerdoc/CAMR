\begin{abstract}
\noindent
This note records the two-phase model \emph{as the code actually solves it},
rather than in its textbook form.  Each equation, closure and source term is
anchored to \code{file:line}.  Runtime options are given with their defaults,
because several of the model's decisions are dials rather than constants.  Two
places where the implementation is known to depart from the model it intends
are flagged explicitly in \S\ref{sec:caveats}.
\end{abstract}

% =====================================================================
\section{State vector}
% =====================================================================

The conserved state carries the two-phase slots
\src{Utils/IndexDefines.H} alongside CAMR's ordinary single-fluid
slots \src{:17--18,~74--76}:

\begin{equation}
  \mathbf{U} =
  \bigl(\
    \underbrace{\rho,\ \rho\uvec,\ \rho E,\ \rho e,\ T}_{\text{stock CAMR}},\
    \underbrace{\alpha_1,\ \alpha_1\rho_1,\ \alpha_2\rho_2,\
                \alpha_1\rho_1E_1,\ \alpha_2\rho_2E_2}_{\text{PS extension}}\
  \bigr)^{\!\top}.
\end{equation}

Write $m_k=\alpha_k\rho_k$ for the partial masses and
$\mathcal{E}_k=\alpha_k\rho_kE_k$ for the phase total energies, with
$E_k=e_k+\tfrac12|\uvec|^2$.  The volume fractions close as
$\alpha_1+\alpha_2=1$, so only $\alpha_1$ is stored.

\paragraph{The state is deliberately redundant.}
Mixture mass and energy are stored \emph{both} as $\rho,\ \rho E$ \emph{and}
as the phase sums.  The two identities
\begin{equation}
  \rho \;=\; m_1+m_2,
  \qquad
  \rho E \;=\; \mathcal{E}_1+\mathcal{E}_2
  \label{eq:identities}
\end{equation}
are therefore invariants to be \emph{maintained}, not consequences of the
discretisation.  They are re-established every step by
\code{ps\_resync\_mixture\_mass} and \code{ps\_resync\_phase\_energy}
\src{CAMR\_advance.cpp} and monitored as checks V4 and V5 of
\code{ps\_validate\_state}.  Historically the mass identity drifted at
$\sim\!0.4\,\%$ per production run before anyone was watching it.

\section{Governing equations}
% =====================================================================

With a single mixture velocity $\uvec$ (velocity equilibrium) and
instantaneous mechanical relaxation, the system solved is

\begin{align}
  \dd{\alpha_1}{t} + \uvec\!\cdot\!\nabla\alpha_1
    &= \mathcal{S}_{\alpha}, \label{eq:alpha}\\[2pt]
  \dd{m_1}{t} + \nabla\!\cdot\!\bigl(m_1\uvec\bigr)
    &= \Gamma, \label{eq:m1}\\[2pt]
  \dd{m_2}{t} + \nabla\!\cdot\!\bigl(m_2\uvec\bigr)
    &= -\,\Gamma, \label{eq:m2}\\[2pt]
  \dd{(\rho\uvec)}{t}
    + \nabla\!\cdot\!\bigl(\rho\,\uvec\!\otimes\!\uvec + P\,\Ivec\bigr)
    &= \mathbf{0}, \label{eq:mom}\\[2pt]
  \dd{\mathcal{E}_1}{t}
    + \nabla\!\cdot\!\bigl(\mathcal{E}_1\uvec + \alpha_1P_1\uvec\bigr)
    &= -\,P_{\!I}\,\dd{\alpha_1}{t}
       \;+\;\mathcal{Q}\;+\;\Gamma H_{\!I}, \label{eq:E1}\\[2pt]
  \dd{\mathcal{E}_2}{t}
    + \nabla\!\cdot\!\bigl(\mathcal{E}_2\uvec + \alpha_2P_2\uvec\bigr)
    &= +\,P_{\!I}\,\dd{\alpha_1}{t}
       \;-\;\mathcal{Q}\;-\;\Gamma H_{\!I}, \label{eq:E2}
\end{align}
%
together with the mixture total energy in strict conservation form,
\begin{equation}
  \dd{(\rho E)}{t}
    + \nabla\!\cdot\!\bigl[(\rho E + P)\,\uvec\bigr] \;=\; 0,
  \label{eq:etot}
\end{equation}
which is the quantity that is fluxed and refluxed, with
\eqref{eq:identities} tying it to \eqref{eq:E1}--\eqref{eq:E2}.  The mixture
pressure is the volume-fraction average
\begin{equation}
  P \;=\; \alpha_1P_1 + \alpha_2P_2,
  \qquad\qquad \src{PS\_hllc.H}
  \label{eq:pmix}
\end{equation}
and the interfacial pressure is the arithmetic mean
\begin{equation}
  P_{\!I} \;=\; \tfrac12\,(P_1+P_2).
  \qquad\qquad \src{hem\_pelanti\_shyue.H}
  \label{eq:pint}
\end{equation}

Equations \eqref{eq:alpha}, \eqref{eq:E1} and \eqref{eq:E2} are
\emph{not} in divergence form: $\alpha_1$ is advected rather than fluxed, and
the phase energies carry the non-conservative product
$P_{\!I}\,\partial_t\alpha_1$.  That is the entire reason the code uses
wave propagation rather than flux differencing for those three slots
(\S\ref{sec:disc}).

\section{Equation of state and the branch-locked contract}
% =====================================================================

The reference backend is Peng--Robinson for pure CO$_2$
($T_c=304.13$~K, $P_c=7.3773\times10^6$~Pa, $\omega=0.22394$).  Queries are
\emph{branch-locked}: the caller nominates the phase, and the EOS returns that
branch, continued metastably past its saturation limit if necessary.  It never
auto-detects, because a nominal vapour sitting at compressed-liquid density
would otherwise return the liquid root and drive the relaxation toward an
unphysical equilibrium.

The corresponding anti-requirement is load-bearing:
\begin{quote}
  \emph{the hydraulic path must not ask the EOS about a mixture state.}
\end{quote}
For a two-phase cell the pair $(\rho,e)$ combines the light phase's volume
with the heavy phase's mass and need not be a single-fluid state at all, so the
inversion may have no root.  Temperature inversion brackets
$T\in[1,5000]$~K and \emph{refuses} outside it rather than returning the
nearest reachable state.

\section{Source terms}
\label{sec:sources}
% =====================================================================

The default relaxation is the \textbf{coupled X3 operator}
(\code{ps\_relax\_mode}$=5$, the default and the acceptance configuration;
an unrecognised or retired mode \emph{aborts} rather than falling
through \src{PS\_relax.H}).  Per reaction substep
\src{CAMR\_advance.cpp}:
\begin{equation}
  \text{hydro}\ \longrightarrow\
  \underbrace{\text{vanish fold}}_{\text{phase death}}\ \longrightarrow\
  \underbrace{\text{X3}}_{\substack{P_1=P_2\ \text{DAE}\ +\ \text{thermal}\ +\\
                                    \text{BE-SRT mass transfer}}}\ \longrightarrow\
  \underbrace{\text{flash}}_{\text{nucleation}}\ \longrightarrow\
  \underbrace{\text{mech.\ reproject}}_{\text{unconditional}}
  \label{eq:chain}
\end{equation}
X3 solves the cell as a constrained system: mechanical equilibrium
$P_1\!=\!P_2$ is imposed as a DAE constraint while the thermal and
mass-transfer legs run as backward-Euler rates \emph{on that constrained
manifold} --- one coupled solve, not a sequence, so the operators cannot
fight over $\alpha_1$.  Its 0-D fixed point is the full-equilibrium (HEM)
flash.  The Newton inside X3 starts from the backward-Euler solution of the
linearised thermal channel, $q^{(0)}=\Delta t\,r_T^0/(1+\Delta t/\theta)$,
and accepts a trial only if it is admissible and does not increase the
scaled residual $|R_1|/E+|R_2|/\rho$ (halving toward the current iterate
otherwise); without the damping the undamped step two-cycles across the
root for most calls \src{hem\_relax\_x3.H}.  The historical sequential
chain (mode 4) and the P+T equilibrium (mode 1) are retired and abort;
mode 0 is mechanical-only (the frozen limit) and must be asked for
explicitly; mode 2 (isochoric $P$, finite-rate thermal, split Gibbs-driven
transfer) is retained as the finite-rate control closure.  In mode 5 the
split mass-transfer source is \emph{owned by X3}: enabling it separately
aborts \src{CAMR\_advance.cpp}.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection{Mechanical relaxation: $\mathcal{S}_\alpha$}
% ---------------------------------------------------------------

Pressure equilibrium is \textbf{instantaneous by model closure}: $\alpha_1$ is
the free variable and is driven until $P_1=P_2$.  It is a constraint, not a
rate.  The accompanying interfacial work is applied to the phase energies
\src{hem\_pelanti\_shyue.H}:
\begin{equation}
  e_1 \;\mapsto\; e_1 - \frac{P_{\!I}\,\Delta\alpha_1}{\alpha_1\rho_1},
  \qquad
  e_2 \;\mapsto\; e_2 + \frac{P_{\!I}\,\Delta\alpha_1}{\alpha_2\rho_2},
  \label{eq:mechwork}
\end{equation}
equal and opposite in $\mathcal{E}_k$, so mixture energy is preserved
exactly.  This is the discrete realisation of $\mp P_{\!I}\partial_t\alpha_1$
in \eqref{eq:E1}--\eqref{eq:E2}.  The Newton step on $\alpha_1$ is capped at a
fraction of $\min(\alpha_1,1-\alpha_1)$ and restricted to the interval where
both $\rho_k$ remain inside the EOS domain.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection{Thermal relaxation: $\mathcal{Q}$}
% ---------------------------------------------------------------

Finite-rate, isochoric, at the $\alpha_1$ the mechanical pass chose.  It
redistributes the energy split only, driving $T_1\!\to\!T_2$ at rate
$1/\theta$ \src{hem\_pelanti\_shyue.H, ps\_iso\_thermal\_relax\_cell\_finite}:
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{E}_k^{\,n+1}
    = \mathcal{E}_k^{\,*}
    + \bigl(\mathcal{E}_k^{\,\mathrm{eq}}-\mathcal{E}_k^{\,*}\bigr)
      \Bigl(1-e^{-\Delta t/\theta}\Bigr),
  \label{eq:thermal}
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal{E}^{\mathrm{eq}}$ is the isochoric $T_1\!=\!T_2$ state.  Note
the form: this is an \emph{exponential relaxation onto a precomputed
equilibrium}.  \code{CAMR.ps\_theta\_tau} \textbf{defaults to
$10^{-7}$~s} --- near-instantaneous at
hydrodynamic scales --- and its semantics are: $\theta>0$ a finite
backward-Euler rate; $\theta\le0$ \emph{instantaneous} thermal
equilibrium, i.e.\ in mode 5 the constraint $T_1\!=\!T_2$ joins the
$P_1\!=\!P_2$ DAE (``$\theta\le0$ does not mean off''
\src{PS\_relax.H}).

The leg is gated by a two-phase coexistence test --- both phase temperatures
strictly inside $(T_{\mathrm{triple}},T_{\mathrm{crit}}) = (216.592,\,304.13)$~K
--- shared with mass transfer and the flash.  \code{CAMR.ps\_coexist\_action}
(default $0$) selects what happens when a phase leaves that band: $0$ silent
no-op, $1$ abort, $2$ run the thermal leg for low-side exits, $3$ run it for
any exit.  The gate carries a \textbf{runaway clause}
\src{hem\_pelanti\_shyue.H, PS\_relaxation.H}: a \emph{high-side-only}
band exit with a phase beyond $2\,T_{\mathrm{crit}}$ ($608$~K for CO$_2$; the
factor is a constexpr on the EOS-supplied $T_{\mathrm{crit}}$, so it is
fluid-agnostic) runs the thermal leg regardless, because the alternative ---
vetoing thermal while the mechanical projection keeps pumping interfacial
work into a vanishing sliver --- was measured to ratchet $e_2$ to the EOS band
edge at the jet contact.  Temperature is the discriminator because it
separates the two populations with wide margins: the hottest legitimate phase
in the validated suite is B10's vapour at $400$~K $=1.32\,T_{\mathrm{crit}}$,
whereas the runaway ratchets through $2\,T_{\mathrm{crit}}$ on its way to
$16\,T_{\mathrm{crit}}$; a mass-trace discriminator was measured and rejected,
since B4/B10's own smeared contact edges hold trace-mass cells below the
sliver's mass ratio.  Material cross-critical contacts
($\lesssim1.5\,T_{\mathrm{crit}}$) keep their veto.  The clause is
bit-inert on the 1-D battery and drains the sliver periodically on the
flashing-vent reference instead of the monotone ratchet.  Runaway activations
are counted (\code{n\_exit\_runaway}, exit-code bit 8) and printed in
\code{[PS-COEXIT-TH]}.

(The per-cell HRM-correlation option \code{CAMR.ps\_theta\_model}$=1$ ---
Downar-Zapolski $\theta=\theta_0\alpha_v^{-0.54}\psi^{-1.76}$ --- was
\textbf{retired}: measured to be the wrong mapping for
\emph{thermal} contact between phases, since the correlation slows toward
saturation, which is right for phase change and wrong for heat exchange;
B9 aborts under it.  The correlation itself remains relevant to mass
transfer; see \S\ref{sec:caveats}(ii).)

% ---------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection{Mass transfer: $\Gamma$ and $H_{\!I}$}
% ---------------------------------------------------------------

Driven by the Gibbs free-energy difference $g_1-g_2$ with $g_k=h_k-T_ks_k$,
and integrated as an exact relaxation onto the instantaneous mass-transfer
equilibrium:
\begin{equation}
  \delta m \;=\; \Bigl(1-e^{-\Delta t/\tau}\Bigr)\,\delta m_{\mathrm{eq}},
  \qquad
  \delta m_{\mathrm{eq}}:\ g_1 = g_2 ,
  \label{eq:mt}
\end{equation}
so $\Gamma = -\delta m/\Delta t$ in \eqref{eq:m1}.  The target
$\delta m_{\mathrm{eq}}$ is found by a Newton iteration on $\delta m$.  The
form \eqref{eq:mt} is unconditionally stable and cannot overshoot
$\delta m_{\mathrm{eq}}$ --- \emph{provided} the target is the equilibrium of
the path actually integrated; see \S\ref{sec:caveats}.

\paragraph{The default rate is derived, not tuned (BE-SRT).}
At the default (mode 5, \code{ps\_mt\_tau\_model}$=2$, ASY1) no relaxation
time is chosen: $\tau$ in \eqref{eq:mt} is \emph{computed} as the distance
to equilibrium over the instantaneous physical rate, per Lund \& Aursand,
\begin{equation}
  \tau_{\mathrm{asy}} = \frac{|\delta m_{\mathrm{eq}}|}{\Gamma_{\!\mathrm{SRT}}},
  \qquad
  \Gamma_{\!\mathrm{SRT}}
    = \Sigma\,\rho_g\sqrt{\frac{M}{2\pi R_u T_g}}\;\bigl|g_1-g_2\bigr|,
  \qquad
  \Sigma = \frac{16}{\pi}\,
           \frac{(\alpha_{\mathrm{recv}}+\delta)\,\alpha_{\mathrm{send}}}{D},
  \label{eq:srt}
\end{equation}
\src{hem\_pelanti\_shyue.H}: the kinetic prefactor is
parameter-free statistical rate theory; the \emph{interfacial-area} closure
$\Sigma$ is not --- it carries the stratified-pipe morphology placeholder
$D=$ \code{CAMR.ps\_mt\_srt\_d} (default $0.1$~m) and the receiving-phase
seed $\delta=$ \code{CAMR.ps\_mt\_srt\_delta} (default $0.01$).  A
degenerate $\Gamma$ falls back to the constant-$\tau$ form so the dial can
never invent a rate.  The morphology dependence this hides is quantified in
\S\ref{sec:caveats}(ii).  The constant \code{ps\_mt\_tau} route belongs to
the non-default modes.

Transferred mass carries the interface total enthalpy
\begin{equation}
  H_{\!I} = h_{\!I} + \tfrac12|\uvec|^2,
  \qquad
  h_{\!I} =
  \begin{cases}
    (1-w)\,h_1 + w\,h_2, & w \ge 0 \quad(w=\tfrac12 \text{ default: mean}),\\[2pt]
    h_1 \ \text{if}\ \delta m>0,\ \ h_2 \ \text{otherwise}, & w<0 \quad(\text{upwind}),
  \end{cases}
  \label{eq:carrier}
\end{equation}
selected by \code{CAMR.ps\_mt\_h\_weight}.

\paragraph{Volume co-moves with the mass (E.1).}
Under the presence model the transfer moves volume at the \emph{donor's own}
density:
\begin{equation}
  \Delta\alpha_1 \;=\; -\,\frac{\delta m}{\rho_1}.
  \label{eq:E1rule}
\end{equation}
Two consequences make this the derived choice rather than a convenience.  The
donor's density is then \emph{exactly} invariant,
$\rho_1' = (m_1-\delta m)/(\alpha_1-\delta m/\rho_1) = \rho_1$, so it can never
leave the EOS domain from below; and the receiver's density becomes a convex
combination (a mediant) of $\rho_2$ and $\rho_1$, so it can never leave from
above.  The proof is rate-independent: even $\delta m = m_1$ in one step lands
$\alpha_1$ exactly on zero.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection{Flash: nucleation only}
% ---------------------------------------------------------------

The flash is \textbf{not} a conversion operator.  It seeds a minority phase up
to $\alpha_{\mathrm{birth}} = 4\times10^{-2}$ (\code{PsPres::alpha\_birth},
single-sourced from the presence parameters, \S\ref{sec:presence}) and
declines once that level is reached \src{hem\_pelanti\_shyue.H, ps\_flash\_source\_cell}:
\begin{equation}
  \alpha_2 \;\mapsto\; \alpha_2 + \beta\,\bigl(\alpha_{\mathrm{birth}}-\alpha_2\bigr),
  \qquad
  \text{return if } \alpha_2^{\,\mathrm{new}} \le \alpha_2 ,
\end{equation}
with $\beta$ a blend of a metastability window, a dominance ramp and
$\Delta t/\tau_f$.  Newborn mass is injected at the \emph{saturation} density
$\rho_{\mathrm{sat}}$, which is \eqref{eq:E1rule} run backwards, so birth and
death are the same operator with opposite sign.  Past
$\alpha_{\mathrm{birth}}$, conversion is mass transfer's job alone.

% =====================================================================
\section{Presence: when a phase has a state at all}
\label{sec:presence}
% =====================================================================

The intensive quantities are quotients with \emph{different} denominators,
\begin{equation}
  \rho_k = \frac{m_k}{\alpha_k},
  \qquad
  e_k = \frac{\mathcal{E}_k}{m_k} - \tfrac12|\uvec|^2,
\end{equation}
so a test on one does not license the other.  Because the hydro advances $m_k$
with an absolute error set by the \emph{mixture} scale, $\rho_k$ inherits a
relative error $\sim\eta/\alpha_k$.  A phase is therefore assigned one of three
regimes by $\alpha_k$ alone --- no auxiliary flag array, so the regime cannot
fall out of step with the state:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
regime & $\alpha_k$ & meaning \\
\midrule
\textsc{absent} & exactly $0$
  & no intensive quantity is ever formed; the cell is single-phase Euler \\
\textsc{corridor} & $(\alpha_{\mathrm{van}},\alpha_{\mathrm{cond}})$
  & transport-only: advects, but no EOS query, no relaxation, no transfer \\
\textsc{independent} & $[\alpha_{\mathrm{cond}},1-\alpha_{\mathrm{cond}}]$
  & a full six-equation phase; every operator acts \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent
with
$\alpha_{\mathrm{cond}}=2\times10^{-2}$ (a \emph{conditioning} bound, derived as
$\eta_{\max}/\varepsilon$ from a measured per-step $\eta_{\max}=5.2\times10^{-4}$
at a $5\,\%$ accuracy target), $\alpha_{\mathrm{van}}=10^{-8}$ (death), and
$\alpha_{\mathrm{birth}}=2\alpha_{\mathrm{cond}}$ (nucleation seed).  The
ordering gives hysteresis with no flip-flop channel: birth at
$2\alpha_{\mathrm{cond}}$, loss of independence only below
$\alpha_{\mathrm{cond}}$, death only below $\alpha_{\mathrm{van}}$.

\paragraph{Promotion degeneracy floor and relax-gate hysteresis.}
Volume fraction alone does not license promotion.  In the corridor
$\alpha_1$ is advected non-conservatively at $S_M$ while $m_1$ is fluxed
conservatively and no source re-couples them, so the two slots drift apart
freely in rough flow; a phase can arrive at $\alpha_k\ge\alpha_{\mathrm{cond}}$
with $\rho_k=m_k/\alpha_k$ far below anything its branch can answer
(measured: a promotion at $\rho_1=0.076$~kg/m$^3$, $e_1=7.6\times10^{7}$~J/kg,
which the branch-locked inversion rightly refused).  The regime classifier
\code{ps\_regime} therefore requires, in addition, $m_k>\rho_{\mathrm{deg}}\,\alpha_k$
(division-free) with $\rho_{\mathrm{deg}}=$ \code{CAMR.ps\_presence\_rho\_deg}
$=0.5$~kg/m$^3$; otherwise the phase stays \textsc{corridor} whatever its
$\alpha_k$.  The value is fixed by two measurements: the minimum legitimate
independent-phase density over all battery finals is $1.406$~kg/m$^3$ (C3) and
the degenerate promotion carried $0.076$, so $0.5$ separates them by a factor
three each way, and zero battery-final cells trip it.  The same floor feeds
two conservative reaps in the vanish fold (a corridor phase with
$m_k\le\rho_{\mathrm{deg}}\alpha_k$, or a phase with $e_k$ outside
$[\code{ps\_presence\_e\_deg\_lo},\code{ps\_presence\_e\_deg\_hi}]=[-2\times10^{6},2\times10^{7}]$~J/kg,
is folded into its host with mass and energy conserved; counted
\code{n\_cdeg}/\code{n\_edeg} in \code{[PS-FOLD]}).  Checked promotion
(\code{CAMR.ps\_promote\_checked}$=1$, \code{PS\_promote.H}) additionally
demotes a would-be independent phase whose energy quotient defines no state
on its own branch.  Finally the relaxation gate carries hysteresis
(\code{CAMR.ps\_relax\_hyst}$=1$, \code{ps\_presence\_relax\_gate}): a phase in
the upper corridor, $\alpha_k\ge\alpha_{\mathrm{cond}}/2$ with
$m_k>\rho_{\mathrm{deg}}\alpha_k$, still counts as relaxable, so the source
on/off boundary no longer coincides with the exact $\alpha$ the relaxation
itself moves (the driver of near-threshold regime toggling in 2-D
production).  The floor and reaps are bit-inert on the 1-D battery; the
hysteresis moves four near-threshold cases by at most $+6.7\,\%$ in a P-error
norm (B11) and is kept for the 2-D behaviour (\code{ps\_relax\_hyst} is
retired; the band is always on in host builds).  The hysteresis band is evaluated on the host only.

% =====================================================================
\section{Discretisation of the non-conservative slots}
\label{sec:disc}
% =====================================================================

The interior scheme is \textbf{single-path}: wave
propagation (\code{CAMR.ps\_flux}$=$\code{wp}, the Berger--LeVeque
fluctuation form) is the only interior flux.  \code{llf}, \code{hllc}, a
set \code{ps\_ctu}, \code{ps\_recon} or \code{ps\_alpha\_limiter} key all
\emph{abort} with retirement notes \src{PS\_umeth.cpp};
\code{PS\_reconstruction.H} is deleted --- the wp interior works from raw
cell averages.  Under AMR the fluctuation form refluxes through the
standard register with $\alpha$ moved alongside
(\code{CAMR.ps\_bl\_reflux}, on by default; any nonzero value is the $\alpha$ co-move).

One Riemann solve per face produces a wave decomposition (retained in
\code{PS\_hllc.H}, which now serves the wp fluctuations), from which the
code takes \emph{two different exits}.

\paragraph{Conserved slots} ($\rho,\rho\uvec,\rho E,m_1,m_2$) receive a single
recovered interface flux $F^\star = F(\mathbf{U}_L) + \mathsf{A}^-\Delta Q$,
which telescopes exactly to the fluctuation update, so CAMR's ordinary
flux-difference path and its AMReX flux register are used unchanged.

\paragraph{Non-conserved slots} ($\alpha_1,\mathcal{E}_1,\mathcal{E}_2$) get a
face flux of \emph{exactly zero} and a direct fluctuation deposit,
\begin{equation}
  \frac{\mathrm{d}\alpha_1}{\mathrm{d}t}\Big|_i
   \;=\;-\,\frac{\mathsf{A}^+\Delta Q_{i-1/2}
                + \mathsf{A}^-\Delta Q_{i+1/2}}{\Delta x},
\end{equation}
and likewise for $\mathcal{E}_1,\mathcal{E}_2$.  Because both exits are fed by
the same wave structure they cannot disagree about what happened at the face,
which is what makes a uniform-pressure, uniform-velocity interface drift
without generating spurious waves \emph{by construction} rather than by
cancellation between two independent discretisations.

\subsection{Wave structure}

Three waves per face (HLLC, specialised to the six-equation system following
Pelanti 2022).  Outer speeds are Davis estimates using each side's own mixture
sound speed \src{PS\_hllc.H}:
\begin{equation}
  S_L = \min\!\bigl(u_L-c_L,\ u_R-c_R\bigr),
  \qquad
  S_R = \max\!\bigl(u_L+c_L,\ u_R+c_R\bigr),
\end{equation}
with the Wallis frozen mixture speed \src{PS\_hllc.H}
\begin{equation}
  c^2 = Y_1c_1^2 + Y_2c_2^2,
  \qquad
  Y_1 = \frac{\alpha_1\rho_1}{\rho},\quad Y_2 = 1-Y_1 .
  \label{eq:cmix}
\end{equation}
``Frozen'' means no mass transfer during the acoustic response; the much
slower equilibrium speed belongs to the relaxation operator, which is applied
separately.  (\code{CAMR.ps\_cmix\_model}, which offered $c=\max(c_1,c_2)$
and the Wood equilibrium form, was measured and \textbf{retired}: MAX gains 3\,\% on a contact because $S_L,S_R$ only bound the
fan, and WOOD aborts B9 with a real-fluid EOS
\src{PS\_presence.H}.  The frozen mass-weighted form is now the
only form.)

The contact speed follows from the mixture mass and momentum balance
\src{PS\_hllc.H}:
\begin{equation}
  S_M = \frac{P_R-P_L + \rho_Lu_L(S_L-u_L) - \rho_Ru_R(S_R-u_R)}
             {\rho_L(S_L-u_L) - \rho_R(S_R-u_R)} .
\end{equation}
$\alpha_1$ is advected at $S_M$ --- the local contact velocity at that face,
not a cell-averaged mixture velocity --- which is what preserves the contact.

\subsection{Star states}
\label{sec:star}

Across each acoustic wave the mixture Rankine--Hugoniot conditions fix one
contraction ratio per side \src{PS\_hllc.H, ps\_star\_state}:
\begin{equation}
  r_K = \frac{S_K-u_K}{S_K-S_M},
  \qquad
  m_1^\star = r_K\,m_1,
  \qquad
  m_2^\star = r_K\,m_2 ,
  \qquad
  u^\star = S_M .
  \label{eq:rk}
\end{equation}
The partial-mass scaling is \emph{forced}: with one velocity and $\alpha$
jumping only at the contact, per-phase mass conservation across a wave moving
at $S_K$ gives $m_k^\star(S_K-S_M)=m_k(S_K-u_K)$ for each phase separately,
so composition is untouched by the acoustic compression.  What the mixture
algebra does \emph{not} fix is the volume partition.  The frozen closure
(Pelanti 2022, eq.~B.14) freezes $\alpha$ across acoustic waves, which forces
\emph{equal volumetric strain} $\rho_k^\star=\rho_k r_K$ on both phases; the
model's own closure then repairs that intermediate by instantaneous mechanical
relaxation immediately afterwards.  A corridor phase (\S\ref{sec:presence}) is
handed the compression but denied the repair, and the equal strain
over-compresses the stiff phase by an order of magnitude at a two-phase wall
reflection.  The code therefore embeds the relaxed intermediate in the star
state itself.

\paragraph{The relaxed-$\alpha$ construction.}
Behind each wave the two phases sit at mechanical equilibrium at a common
pressure rise $\mathrm{d}P$, and each takes the strain its own bulk modulus
$B_k=\rho_kc_k^2$ admits:
\begin{equation}
  s_k = \frac{\mathrm{d}P}{B_k},
  \qquad
  \rho_k^\star = \rho_k\,(1+s_k),
  \qquad
  \alpha_k^\star = \frac{m_k^\star}{\rho_k^\star}
                 = \frac{\alpha_k\,r_K}{1+s_k},
  \label{eq:strain}
\end{equation}
and volume saturation $\alpha_1^\star+\alpha_2^\star=1$ becomes one scalar
equation in $\mathrm{d}P$,
\begin{equation}
  f(\mathrm{d}P)
  = \frac{\alpha_1 r_K}{1+\mathrm{d}P/B_1}
  + \frac{\alpha_2 r_K}{1+\mathrm{d}P/B_2} - 1 = 0 ,
  \label{eq:volclosure}
\end{equation}
which is monotone decreasing wherever $1+s_k>0$, so its root is unique.  The
seed is the linearisation of \eqref{eq:volclosure},
$\mathrm{d}P_0=(1-1/r_K)\big/\bigl(\alpha_1/B_1+\alpha_2/B_2\bigr)$ --- the
mixture volumetric strain distributed by the Wood (mechanical-equilibrium)
compressibility, which is precisely what ``relaxed'' means --- followed by
\emph{three} Newton steps, a fixed count with no convergence threshold to
tune.  The energy partition keeps the kinetic term and pays each phase
$P\,\mathrm{d}v$ for its \emph{own} volume change,
\begin{equation}
  E_k^\star = E_k + (S_M-u_K)\,S_M
            + P\,\frac{s_k}{\rho_k\,(1+s_k)},
  \qquad
  \frac{s_k}{\rho_k(1+s_k)} = \frac{1}{\rho_k}-\frac{1}{\rho_k^\star},
  \label{eq:estar}
\end{equation}
with $P$ the \emph{mixture} pressure \eqref{eq:pmix}.  (The per-phase
alternative \code{CAMR.ps\_pk\_energy\_flux}$=1$ is retired; a set key
aborts.)  Whenever \eqref{eq:volclosure} is solved exactly,
$\sum_k m_k^\star E_k^\star = r_K\bigl[\rho E+\rho(S_M-u_K)S_M\bigr]+P\,(r_K-1)$,
term for term the B.14 mixture sum, so the mixture energy Rankine--Hugoniot
condition holds \emph{exactly} and every conserved-slot star component
($\rho$, momenta, $\rho E$, $\rho e$, both partial masses, species) is
identical between the two constructions: the conservative flux route is
untouched, wave speeds and $\Delta t$ are untouched, and the change is
confined to the three non-conserved slots' wave components, where $\alpha_1$
now jumps across the acoustic waves too (the Kapila compression term,
discretely) and the phase energies are re-partitioned.  No new constant enters:
$c_k$, $\alpha_k$, $\rho_k$, $P$, $r_K$, $S_M$ are all already in the face
state.

\paragraph{Limits.}
Equal bulk moduli ($B_1=B_2$, which includes every single-phase cell and the
$c$-slaved corridor face with $\rho_1=\rho_2$) give $1+s_k=r_K$ for both
phases, $\alpha_k^\star=\alpha_k$, $\rho_k^\star=\rho_kr_K$, and the work term
reduces to $(S_M-u_K)P/q_k$ with $q_k=\rho_k(S_K-u_K)$: B.14 is the
equal-compressibility special case.  A zero-strength wave ($S_M=u_K$,
$r_K=1$) gives $\mathrm{d}P=0$ and star $=$ cell state, so the uniform
interface and the identity case are fixed points.  As $\alpha_1\to0$ the
trace phase's strain scales by $B_2/B_1$; with the host-slaved corridor
sound speed that ratio is $\rho_2/\rho_1$.  Exact $\alpha_1\in\{0,1\}$ skips
the solve, since the forms coincide there and the presence design needs those
values preserved bit-exactly.  The fan geometry ($S_L$, $S_R$ from the Wallis
frozen speed) stays frozen-acoustic while the internal partition is relaxed;
this mirrors what the cell update does (frozen hydro, then instantaneous
relaxation) and leaves upwinding and $\Delta t$ alone.

\paragraph{Degeneracy.}
A non-finite or vanishing bulk modulus, $1+s_k\le0$ at any Newton iterate
(a rarefaction past a phase's linear-acoustic range), a non-finite iterate,
or a root with $\alpha_1^\star\notin(0,1)$ makes the face fall back to the
equal-strain B.14 partition for that face --- the model's own weak form, a
principled fallback rather than a clamp --- and is counted
(\code{face\_diag::n\_relax\_fb}); the measured fallback rate is zero across
the 1-D suite and 225 steps of 2-D production.  Measured on acceptance: the
single-phase A/C battery and the identity case move by exactly nothing; the
two-phase wall reflection B12 drops from a reflection ratio $c_1/c_2=1.0000$
to $0.0097$ (gate $\le0.25$); the both-phases-independent B5 keeps the
energy identity to $6\times10^{-14}$ with zero fallbacks.

\paragraph{Failure is refusal, not repair.}
A non-finite intermediate, a vanishing denominator or a non-positive star
density makes the face return \code{false}, and that face alone falls back to a
local Lax--Friedrichs flux.  Nothing is clamped to make the star state exist.

\subsection{Second-order correction and the contact-wave taper}
\label{sec:limiter}

At \code{CAMR.ps\_wp\_order}$=2$ (the acceptance order, set by every
deck; the compiled default is $1$) each wave carries a van Leer-limited
Lax--Wendroff correction flux (\code{CAMR.ps\_wp\_limiter}, \code{vanleer};
\code{none} gives the unlimited correction for smooth order-of-accuracy
tests only).  The limiter ratio is one scalar per wave, formed from a
nondimensionalised projection of the wave onto its upwind neighbour
(\code{CAMR.ps\_wp\_proj\_scale}$=1$), so the per-wave linear identities
$W[\rho]=W[m_1]+W[m_2]$ and $W[\rho E]=W[\mathcal{E}_1]+W[\mathcal{E}_2]$
survive limiting: scaling a wave by a scalar preserves them, whereas limiting
slots individually would not.  The contact wave ($S_M$) carries the
non-conservative $\alpha$ jump, and correcting it at a genuine material
interface smears $\alpha$ into the phase densities and energies; dropping the
correction everywhere costs single-phase contact sharpness (A/C battery mean
$0.0350\to0.0374$).  The code therefore scales the whole contact-wave
correction by one face weight \src{PS\_umeth.cpp},
\begin{equation}
  w_c = 1 - \mathrm{smoothstep}\!\left(
        \frac{|\alpha_{1,L}-\alpha_{1,R}|}{\alpha_{\mathrm{cond}}}\right),
  \qquad
  \mathrm{smoothstep}(t)=t^2(3-2t),\ \ t\in[0,1],
  \label{eq:wckeep}
\end{equation}
(\code{CAMR.ps\_lw\_skip\_contact}$=2$, the default): $|\Delta\alpha|=0$ ---
uniform trace or single-phase --- keeps the full correction and the A/C mean
$0.0350$ exactly; $|\Delta\alpha|\ge\alpha_{\mathrm{cond}}$, a material
interface, drops it; the smoothstep between removes the on/off kink that a
hard regime switch imprints on the front at the $\alpha_{\mathrm{cond}}$
contour.  The blend width \emph{is} $\alpha_{\mathrm{cond}}$, so no new
threshold enters; a level-based taper was rejected because the A/C uniform
trace sits below $\alpha_{\mathrm{cond}}$ and would be wrongly skipped, while
the jump-based taper keeps uniform regions at weight one at any level.  On
the 2-D jet the taper removes the $\alpha_{\mathrm{cond}}$ kink, reduces the
near-orifice zigzag about fivefold and halves the spurious extrema.  Modes
$0$ (full correction) and $1$ (blanket drop) remain as A/B opt-outs.

\section{Two known departures of the implementation from the model}
\label{sec:caveats}
% =====================================================================

Recorded here because they are properties of the code, not of the model.
Their status at the shared release:

\paragraph{(i) The mass-transfer target and the step's path (resolved by
default).}
The step applies \eqref{eq:E1rule} with the carrier \eqref{eq:carrier};
historically $\delta m_{\mathrm{eq}}$ in \eqref{eq:mt} was computed at
\emph{fixed} $\alpha_1$ with a \emph{mean} carrier regardless of $w$.
Along fixed $\alpha_1$, $\partial\rho_1/\partial m = 1/\alpha_1$, whereas
along \eqref{eq:E1rule} it is exactly zero, so the target's Gibbs
sensitivity carried a $1/\alpha_1$ amplification the step does not have,
breaking the non-overshoot property of \eqref{eq:mt}.
The consistent target (same carrier and the same (E.1) path as the step,
\src{PS\_sources.H}) is the only target; \code{ps\_mt\_target} is retired.

\paragraph{(ii) The mass-transfer rate carries a morphology assumption ---
now with a measured handle.}
The physical rate is set by the interfacial area the two phases share
\emph{within} a cell; the six-equation state carries no interfacial-area
variable, so the SRT closure \eqref{eq:srt} parameterises it through the
single length $D$.  A dispersed mixture and a numerically smeared contact
can carry identical cell states and still require rates differing by
orders of magnitude; the coexistence gate of \S\ref{sec:sources} acts as a
crude binary proxy for that distinction.  Two measurements now calibrate
the dial (the DT7-4.1 shock tube of SINTEF report
DT7 2019-06): (a) the \emph{effective} HRM relaxation time of the scheme
maps linearly onto the report's $\theta$-axis as
$\theta_{\mathrm{eff}} \approx 0.08\,D$ [s per m], verified over four
decades including the slow-branch topology change, so the default
$D=0.1$~m behaves as $\theta_{\mathrm{eff}}\approx8$~ms; and (b) the
Downar-Zapolski correlation evaluated at that flow's own fan states
prescribes $\theta\sim2\times10^{-5}$~s --- the default morphology
placeholder is $\sim\!2.5$ decades \emph{slower} than the flashing-flow
correlation on that problem.  A DZ-defensible default would be
$D\sim2.5\times10^{-4}$~m; changing it is an open (G-DEF-gated) decision,
not yet taken.

\section{Characteristic outflow (NSCBC) with a flashing ghost}
\label{sec:nscbc}
% =====================================================================

Subsonic characteristic outflow (\code{CAMR.ps\_bc\_use\_nscbc}$=1$;
construction in \code{PS\_nscbc.H}) builds the ghost state from the
outgoing invariant $R^+$ (minmod-extrapolated) and an ambient-encoding
target $R^-_{\mathrm{targ}}=-P_{\mathrm{amb}}/\rho c$ and an optional
term $-\sigma(P_N-P_{\mathrm{amb}})/\rho c$ (\code{CAMR.ps\_bc\_nscbc\_sigma},
default 0.25).  Measured on the pipe-break outlet, the $\sigma$ term is a
\emph{partial reflection}, not the Poinsot--Lele restoring rate it is named
after: the steady face obeys $u=(1-\sigma)(P-P_{\mathrm{amb}})/\rho c$, and
$\sigma=0$ places the face exactly on the outgoing simple wave of a far
field at rest.  Production decks run $\sigma=0$; the key is a retirement
candidate (\code{DESIGN\_DECISIONS.md} B-4).  The ambient pressure is \code{prob.p\_amb}, overridable
\emph{per face} via \code{CAMR.ps\_bc\_p\_amb\_\{x,y,z\}\{lo,hi\}}
(sentinel $-1$ inherits).  The per-phase repack integrates the isentrope
adaptively in pressure sub-steps capped at
$\Delta P \le 0.05\,\rho_k c_k^2$ (one step reproduces the historical
small-signal arithmetic bit-for-bit).  For large exterior jumps the ghost
is a \textbf{choked fan}: the state marches toward $P_{\mathrm{amb}}$
along the per-phase isentropes with HEM saturated re-closure inside the
dome ($T_{\mathrm{sat}}$ by bisection on the EOS $P_{\mathrm{sat}}$;
enthalpy-lever quality), $\mathrm{d}u = -\mathrm{d}P/(\rho c_{\mathrm{path}})$
with $c_{\mathrm{path}}^2 = \mathrm{d}P/\mathrm{d}\rho_{\mathrm{mix}}$
along the path, terminating at the critical-flow throat ($G=\rho u$
maximum) or at $P_{\mathrm{amb}}$, whichever comes first.
The in-dome re-closure is the only ghost construction
(\code{ps\_bc\_nscbc\_flash} is retired).  Measured on the flashing-vent benchmark: vented-mass deficit
$-4.1\,\%$ against a boundary-free reference (the retired legacy
construction sat at $-7.6\,\%$, the frozen one at $-34\,\%$).  The legacy
treatment and its \code{ps\_bc\_nscbc\_v2} key are retired (a set key
aborts).
